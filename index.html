Philadelphia Zone 7b Guide

March/April (Spring Start):

  • Peas, Kale, Lettuce, Spinach, Radishes, Onions.

May (After Last Frost):

  • Tomatoes, Peppers, Eggplant, Cucumbers, Squash, Beans, Basil.

June/July (Summer Heat):

  • Okra, Sweet Potatoes, Corn, Peppers.

August/Sept (Fall Crop):

  • Broccoli, Cabbage, Carrots, Collards, Turnips.

October/Nov (Overwinter):

  • Garlic (Plant now for next summer), Cover crops.
