📅 Seasonal Planting Guide

March – April

  • 🌿 Plant peas, kale, spinach indoors
  • 🥕 Direct sow carrots & beets after last frost
  • 🌱 Start tomato & pepper seedlings indoors
  • ❄️ Watch for late frost below 32°F

May

  • 🍅 Transplant tomatoes & peppers outdoors
  • 🥒 Plant cucumbers, squash, beans
  • 🌽 Direct sow corn after soil warms to 60°F
  • 🌼 Add compost & mulch to all beds

June – July

  • 💧 Water deeply 2–3× per week in heat
  • 🍅 Stake tomatoes & train climbers
  • ✂️ Prune suckers from tomato plants
  • 🌿 Succession sow lettuce every 2 weeks

August

  • 🥬 Plant fall crops: kale, broccoli, chard
  • 🧄 Order garlic for October planting
  • 📸 Log harvest weights for records

September – October

  • 🧄 Plant garlic cloves 2" deep
  • 🥦 Harvest broccoli before hard freeze
  • 🌿 Cover crop with winter rye or clover
  • 🍂 Clear spent plants, add to compost

November

  • 🛡️ Mulch beds 3–4" for winter protection
  • 📋 Plan next year's crop rotation
  • 🪴 Bring tender perennials indoors
